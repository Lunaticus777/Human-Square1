The project

Live by the ocean,
15 minutes from Porto

Horizonte Atlântico is a development of 15 detached villas on Rua do Monte, in the parish union of Gulpilhares and Valadares — an established residential area close to the Atlantic coast.

Set in terraces on a gentle slope, the villas offer privacy between neighbours and excellent sun exposure. An internal road with pavements and visitor parking connects to Rua do Monte and Rua de Cavadão.

High-performance envelope

ETICS external insulation (U ≤ 0.35 W/m²K), thermally broken frames (Uw ≤ 1.4) and a fully waterproofed roof — what can't be fixed after construction.

Technical specification

Reference specifications

A single specification, identical for all 15 villas, signed by the developer and Human Square.

01Structure & foundations
  • Traditional reinforced-concrete construction, foundations sized by geotechnical study
  • Pre-stressed beam-and-block floors; solid slabs for balconies
  • Perimeter drainage with geotextile and waterproofing of buried parts
  • Service ducts built into the structure — no later drilling
02Façades & roof
  • Double brick walls + ETICS external insulation, U ≤ 0.35 W/m²K
  • Vila Real natural stone cladding (2 cm) on the street-side ground floor
  • Flat roof with XPS insulation, two-layer membrane and gravel protection
  • Roof prepared for solar PV / solar thermal panels
03Windows & shutters
  • Thermally broken aluminium, RAL 7016, Uw ≤ 1.4 W/m²K
  • Low-E double glazing with solar control; laminated glass on full-height frames
  • Motorised aluminium roller shutters, per-room control
  • Multi-point security front door; motorised sectional garage door
04Interiors
  • Rectified 60×60 porcelain: hall, kitchen, bathrooms, laundry
  • Engineered flooring with natural wood veneer: living room, hallways, bedrooms
  • Suspended ceilings with shadow gap and curtain recess
  • White lacquered doors, built-in wardrobes with soft-close
05Kitchen
  • 19 mm moisture-resistant MDF units, white lacquered fronts, Blum-type hardware
  • Granite worktop or equivalent
  • Hob, oven, hood, microwave, fridge-freezer and dishwasher
  • Central island where shown on plan
06Bathrooms
  • Fully equipped: wall-hung sanitaryware, shower, vanity units, mirrors, screens
  • Full-height wall tiling
  • Liquid waterproofing under shower areas
  • Personalised selection at partner showroom
07Air conditioning & hot water
  • Reversible Multi-Split Inverter A/C in living room and bedrooms (Daikin, Mitsubishi, Panasonic or equiv.)
  • Hot water by A+ heat pump (200 L)
  • Mechanical extraction in wet rooms
  • Minimum energy class A targeted
08Electrical & smart home
  • 13.8 kVA three-phase installation, panel with 20% spare capacity
  • IP video intercom with night-vision camera
  • Fibre optic, RJ45 sockets in living room and every bedroom
  • Pre-installed: solar PV, EV charger, home automation and 3 camera conduits
09Outdoors
  • Natural granite 10×10 paving for access and parking
  • 60×60 non-slip porcelain terrace (choice of 5 models)
  • Levelled lawned garden, Photinia ‘Red Robin’ hedge
  • RAL 7016 boundary walls for 2 m of privacy
